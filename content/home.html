Welcome to

Hawkins Hollow

Hawkins Hollow is a warm and welcoming world where readers can meet memorable characters, explore beloved places, and discover stories that feel lived in. The neighborhood is built for curious visitors who want to learn, wander, and belong. It is also a place where the books themselves carry the heart of the world into every new page, and we sell these stories so families can bring them home.

Built as a living world

This site treats Hawkins Hollow as a growing place, not a simple catalog of titles. Each page is meant to help visitors feel the warmth, connection, and adventure that define the neighborhood. The result is a welcoming home for characters, places, resources, and the books that help the world keep breathing.
