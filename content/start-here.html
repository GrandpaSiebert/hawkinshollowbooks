Start Here

New visitors can begin gently and discover the many paths into Hawkins Hollow. This page helps orient readers toward the books, characters, and places that make the world feel welcoming and alive. There is no rush here; the goal is simply to invite everyone in and help them find the next story worth exploring.

Welcome

Hawkins Hollow is built to feel like an invitation rather than a maze. Each next step leads to more of the world without overwhelming the visitor, and each path is meant to feel friendly and purposeful. The experience is rooted in curiosity, warmth, and the joy of discovering what belongs in this neighborhood.
