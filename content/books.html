Books

The books of Hawkins Hollow are meant to welcome readers into a neighborhood that feels familiar and alive. Each story adds another layer to the world, whether through a beloved character, a treasured place, or a meaningful family moment. The reading experience is simple, warm, and deeply connected to the charm of the place itself. We also sell these stories so families can bring the magic home with them.

Series overview

These series are organized to make it easy to browse the stories that belong to Hawkins Hollow. Visitors can move from one title to the next and discover the voices, themes, and friendships that shape the larger world. Every addition supports the site’s goal of inviting readers in with clarity, warmth, and a sense of belonging. The collection is designed to feel accessible, welcoming, and ready to enjoy.
