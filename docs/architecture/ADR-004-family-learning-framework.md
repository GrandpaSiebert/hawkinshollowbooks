# ADR-004: Family Learning Framework

## Decision

Product evolution is driven by real family observations, starting with structured early-family learning cycles.

## Why

Hawkins Hollow is a family experience product. Real usage signals from families provide higher-value guidance than speculative technical optimization.

## Alternatives Considered

- Feature roadmap driven primarily by technical opportunity.
- Content roadmap driven primarily by internal preferences without field feedback.

## Consequences

- Success metrics prioritize family outcomes and engagement quality.
- Content production and iteration cadence are informed by observed behavior.
- Infrastructure changes require demonstrated production need.
