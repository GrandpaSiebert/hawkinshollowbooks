# The Hawkins Hollow Promise

This is the hospitality and experience constitution for Hawkins Hollow.

Working definition:

- Hawkins Hollow is a community that introduces itself, one neighbor at a time.

Technical architecture explains how the software works.
This promise explains why the experience exists and how it should feel.

Hawkins Hollow was never meant to be just a website.

It is a place where families are welcomed, curiosity is encouraged, friendships grow, and stories are shared. Every decision we make, whether it involves code, illustrations, writing, or design, should help visitors feel a little more at home than they did a moment before.

## Why This Exists

As Hawkins Hollow grows, features will change.
The welcome should not.

Use this document as a decision filter for design, copy, navigation, and feature behavior.

## The Constitutions

### Technical Constitution

These principles keep the system trustworthy and correct.

- Canonical source files are authoritative.
- Stable entity IDs persist across builds.
- Relationship provenance is preserved on graph edges.
- Build pipelines generate repeatable artifacts.

### Creative Constitution

These principles keep the world recognizable and emotionally consistent.

- Every visit should feel like arriving at the same beloved place on a different day.
- Rotate windows, not truth.
- Discovery should deepen familiarity, never replace it.
- Context is persistent. Navigation is incidental.
- Every click should feel like continuing the same visit.
- Seasonal changes stay gentle, familiar, and story-first.
- Favor small shifts in tone, greeting copy, and suggestions over full redesigns.
- Every page should leave visitors feeling just a little lighter than when they arrived.
- Nothing in Hawkins Hollow should ever feel hurried.

Front porch and living room distinction:

- Front porches introduce with a few curated invitations.
- Living rooms hold complete, reference-quality detail.
- Visitors should always feel they are meeting the same neighbors in both spaces.

### Hospitality Constitution

These principles keep each page welcoming and human.

Every page should answer these questions:

- Do visitors feel welcomed?
- Do they feel curious?
- Do they feel calmer than when they arrived?
- Have we invited exploration instead of directing traffic?
- Have we earned the opportunity to recommend a book?

If the answer to those questions is yes, the page is succeeding.

Introduction test:

- Does this help one neighbor naturally introduce another part of Hawkins Hollow?

### Stewardship Constitution

We are caretakers of Hawkins Hollow, not merely builders of it.

Every story, illustration, page, and feature should leave the world a little richer, a little kinder, and a little more welcoming than it was before.

## How To Use This Promise

Before shipping any page or feature, run a quick review:

1. Confirm technical integrity (data quality, stability, provenance).
2. Confirm creative continuity (same place, different day).
3. Confirm hospitality quality (welcome, calm, curiosity, gentle invitation).

If a change improves function but weakens the welcome, revise the experience before release.

## A Note For Future Contributors

Before changing anything, spend a moment on the front porch.

Remember that every visitor is arriving as a guest, not as a user.

Build features that help them feel welcomed.
Build pages that encourage them to wander.
Build stories that invite them back.

When in doubt, choose the path that makes Hawkins Hollow feel more like home.

Take good care of Hawkins Hollow, and it will take good care of the families who visit.
