# Book discovery review — October 16, 2026

This pass covers the 79 published books with missing discovery categories. Songs and nursery rhymes are deferred. Existing recorded labels remain authoritative and are preserved.

All 79 narratives were read. The Patriot Day book required visual reading of its image-only published PDF. The other 78 were read from their Story Master manuscripts, with illustration prompts, marketing copy and production notes excluded as narrative evidence. Stated source metadata supplies age ranges.

Of the 71 books missing feelings, 47 support significant emotional classifications and 24 remain without a feeling label. The other eight retain their existing feeling labels. Skills/activities were supported for 64 books; 15 comfort or reflective stories remain without an activity label. The two books missing interests and age ranges now have supported labels.

A feeling must be central to the story or a substantial part of its resolution. A passing reaction, reader response or quiet setting is insufficient. Explicit and strongly implied evidence are distinguished in the review data. Skills can be demonstrated in a story or invited in its support pages; a skill label does not promise a downloadable activity.

Each annotation has a rationale, short source quotation, paragraph or PDF page reference, and SHA-256 fingerprint. The build checks Word quotations against the source paragraphs. Image-only PDF quotations were manually checked against the fingerprinted file. Changed or missing sources withhold their annotations and appear in generated/validation/discovery-book-review.json for re-review.

Review data: [discovery-book-annotations.json](../data/discovery-book-annotations.json). These are derived discovery annotations; the Library, KDP workbook and authoritative book data were not edited. Internal evidence is omitted from the public discovery index. A reviewed book without a supported label remains available in the form’s additional unclassified results.
