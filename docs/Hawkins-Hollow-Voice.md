# Hawkins Hollow Voice Guide

This guide defines how Hawkins Hollow sounds.

## Core Voice

Write as if you are walking beside someone, not standing in front of them.

The voice is welcoming, calm, and observant.
It should feel like someone quietly opening the gate and saying, Look who is home.

## Voice Rules

- Speak with visitors, never at them.
- Notice things more often than explaining them.
- Invite more often than instruct.
- Leave room for imagination.
- Never rush anyone.
- Assume every visitor is welcome.

## Seasonal Writing Rule

Grandpa never announces the season.
Grandpa notices what is happening around him.

Use observed details instead of labels.

Prefer:
- The leaves have been taking their time this year.

Avoid:
- It is autumn in Hawkins Hollow.

## Weekly Rhythm Rule

Grandpa thoughts should change slowly.

- Use one thought per week, tied to season.
- Do not rotate randomly on refresh.
- Keep tone consistent with a lived-in place.

## Commerce Tone Rule

Books and resources should feel like a continuation of belonging, not an interruption.

Prefer invitations such as:
- Take a little of Hawkins Hollow home.

Avoid direct pressure language.

## Narration Grammar Rule

Across page types, write in this order:

1. Welcome.
2. Why this matters.
3. What it is.
4. What happens next.

This keeps voice consistent while allowing each neighbor, place, and story to keep its own personality.

Prefer lines that begin with lived meaning.
Reveal labels and technical detail only after the invitation is clear.

## Resource Narration Rule

Resource pages should answer "Why would I want this?" before they answer "What is this?"

This is the first formal application of the broader narration grammar.

Prefer headlines, invitations, and purpose statements that feel concrete and warm.

Use metadata to support the experience, not to lead it.

Prefer:
- A calm guide for the conversation that comes after the story.
- Reading together builds confidence one page at a time.

Avoid:
- Resource Type: Family Discussion Guide
- Resource ID: HH-A-0001-FAM-GUIDE
