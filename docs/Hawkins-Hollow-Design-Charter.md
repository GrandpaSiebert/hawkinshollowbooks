# Hawkins Hollow Design Charter

Adopted: August 2026

## Purpose

Hawkins Hollow exists to leave every visitor a little lighter than when they arrived.

Completion now means a visitor can keep walking naturally, not just keep clicking.

## Principles

1. Every visitor is welcome.
2. Every story deserves its turn to greet.
3. Every page offers one gentle next step.
4. Architecture exists to disappear.
5. Would the Spider have asked for this?

## Stewardship

We build the roads.

We walk the roads.

We tell the stories.

Each role serves the visitor.

## Governance

This charter should remain short enough to read before making any meaningful change.

New behavior should arise from observed visitor experience rather than theoretical capability.

Feature gate: no new feature without a clear principle mapping and a Spider-observed need.

The charter changes only when repeated lived experience reveals a conflict with its principles.

Charter updates are date-based, not versioned. When principles evolve, record a new adoption date and preserve prior charter text in project history.
