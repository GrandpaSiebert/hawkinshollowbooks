# Hawkins Hollow Validation Notes

## Purpose
Use this file to record visitor-focused observations during a fresh walkthrough of the Storybooks journey.

## Validation lenses
- Orientation: Do I know where I am and what kind of place this is?
- Momentum: Do I naturally want to take the next step without searching for it?
- Satisfaction: After several clicks, does this feel like exploring Hawkins Hollow rather than navigating a website?

## Suggested walkthrough
1. Open the homepage.
2. Choose Storybooks.
3. Open a story.
4. Follow the suggested character or place.
5. Continue to the next suggested step.

## Note format
Use one bullet per hesitation:
- Step: where the hesitation happened
- Hesitation: what felt unclear or weak
- Lens: Orientation, Momentum, or Satisfaction
