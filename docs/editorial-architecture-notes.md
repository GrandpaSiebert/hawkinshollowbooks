# Editorial architecture notes

The purpose of the architecture is to disappear.

Books describe books. Series describe editorial guidance. The generator publishes. CSS presents.

Editorial guidance belongs in the series data because it helps visitors know where to begin and how to enter the world of Hawkins Hollow. The generator should not decide that voice for itself.

The next phase is observation. Notice where visitors hesitate, where they smile, where they want to click next, and where the experience feels like a welcome rather than a website.
