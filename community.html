Community

Hawkins Hollow is strongest when readers, families, and neighbors feel welcome to linger and explore together. The community around this world is meant to be warm, curious, and open to new visitors who want to learn more. It is a place where stories are shared, conversations begin, and wonder keeps growing.

Stay connected

This page is where the wider Hawkins Hollow experience can grow through updates, shared discoveries, and a sense of belonging. We want visitors to feel invited to return for more books, more character stories, and more reasons to keep exploring the neighborhood. Whether someone is seeking a new title, a favorite character, or simply a place to feel at home, the community remains open and welcoming.

Come back soon for more stories and updates.
