Start Here

If you are new to Hawkins Hollow, this is the best place to begin. The world is designed to welcome visitors gently and help them find the stories, characters, and places that matter most. Each next step invites discovery without pressure, so readers can follow their curiosity at their own pace.

Where to begin

Start with the story pages, then wander into the character and place guides to deepen your sense of the world. You can also explore the resource page for helpful context and a more complete picture of what is available. The goal is to make everyone feel welcome, informed, and ready to explore what Hawkins Hollow has to offer.
