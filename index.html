Welcome to

Hawkins Hollow Books

Hawkins Hollow is a warm, welcoming place where stories grow from family, curiosity, and everyday wonder. Visitors are invited to meet the characters, wander the places, and discover the resources that make this world feel lived in. We also sell the books that carry the heart of the neighborhood into new reading adventures and family shelves.

What you will find here

This site is designed to welcome new readers and longtime fans into the living world of Hawkins Hollow. You can explore the people who shape its stories, the places that give the neighborhood character, and the resources that help families read, learn, and connect. It is also a place to discover the books that bring these moments to life and to find the next story worth sharing.

Cornerstone pages

Start with the pages that help visitors settle in and feel at home. Each destination is meant to orient readers toward the heart of the world and the joy of discovery. From the first welcome to the next favorite title, every step is shaped to feel friendly, useful, and true to Hawkins Hollow.
